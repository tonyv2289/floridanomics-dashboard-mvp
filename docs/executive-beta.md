# Floridanomics Executive Beta

Floridanomics will validate its executive-intelligence model over three Florida labor-release cycles before broad promotion.

## Cohort

Recruit 15-20 named users across five decision roles:

- state strategy and policy
- regional economic development and site selection
- corporate operators
- investors and capital allocators
- research, workforce, and innovation leaders

The named roster and outreach status live in the private Pelayo Vault. Do not store personal email addresses or private interview notes in this public repository.

## Production gate

Each cycle follows the same sequence:

1. Refresh official data and preserve the previous vintage.
2. Run data validation, lint, type checking, tests, build, and security audit.
3. Review revisions and the generated `What Changed` memo.
4. Review policy and project ledgers for material developments.
5. Add editorial interpretation only after the evidence review.
6. Publish the briefing and dashboard update.
7. Send one tracked cohort link per participant using a non-PII `invite` token.
8. Log replies, forwards, citations, evidence opens, and missing decisions.

## What to measure

Primary behavior proof:

- five or more beta users return, forward, or cite the product without a reminder
- three or more beta users name a decision or work product Floridanomics improved
- evidence and source opens demonstrate that users treat the dashboard as a verification layer

Supporting measures:

- briefing-to-dashboard click-through
- return visits by private invite token
- Today, Competition, Policy, and Evidence view usage
- source, evidence export, and download interactions
- direct referrals to another executive
- interview themes: forwarded, cited, ignored, missing

## Interview prompts

1. What did you forward or cite?
2. What changed a decision, meeting, memo, or investment discussion?
3. What did you ignore?
4. What did you need but could not find?
5. Would you notice if Floridanomics stopped arriving?
6. Which narrow recurring job would be valuable enough to pay for?

## Decision after cycle three

Choose one path based on observed behavior:

- public launch with the monthly executive brief as the primary habit
- continue a controlled beta while repairing trust or distribution gaps
- narrow the product around the strongest repeated job, such as competitor-state monitoring, a board brief, a policy watch, or a site-selection evidence pack
