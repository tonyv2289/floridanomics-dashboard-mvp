About Floridanomics

Floridanomics tracks Florida's economy region by region: jobs, wages, trade and investment, measured where they happen.

  • Every figure is dated and sourced. Each number links to the public agency or publication that reported it, with the date it describes.
  • Facts and views are kept apart. Reported data and editorial analysis are labeled separately. Comparisons between states use common-method sources, and where states count differently, the site says so instead of ranking them.
  • Nonpartisan. Floridanomics covers economic evidence, not candidates or parties.

Who publishes it

Floridanomics is published by Floridanomics, LLC.
