About Floridanomics

Floridanomics tracks Florida's economy region by region: jobs, wages, trade, and investment, measured where they happen.

Who is behind it

I'm TJ Villamil. I served the state from 2021 to 2024 as Senior Vice President at Enterprise Florida, Deputy Secretary of Economic Development at FloridaCommerce, and the first President of International Commerce at SelectFlorida. Since September 2024, I have led business development at eMerge Americas. I'm a double Gator and a former UF student body president.

Floridanomics started in 2012, while I was at UF, as a Florida economy newsletter. I brought it back in 2026 so the people who attract, grow, and retain companies in Florida have the numbers in one place, each with its source and date. It also shows how Florida compares with the states it competes with for major projects.

How it works

Every number links to the public agency or publication that reported it, with the date it describes. Official data and my own analysis are labeled separately. When states count something differently, the site says so instead of ranking them. Floridanomics covers the economy, not candidates or parties.

Who publishes it

Floridanomics is published by Floridanomics, LLC. It is independent of eMerge Americas and of any government agency, and the views here are my own.
